## Ingredients

**Crispy Tofu**
- 14 oz firm tofu, pressed and cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp neutral oil

**Soup**
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2-inch knob fresh ginger, grated
- 2 medium carrots, diced
- 2 stalks celery, diced
- 1 cup brown lentils, rinsed
- 2 cups frozen cauliflower florets
- 5–6 cups water or unsalted vegetable stock
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar, plus more to taste
- Salt and white pepper to taste

**Finish**
- 1 bunch asparagus, woody ends snapped, cut into 1½-inch pieces
- 2 scallions, thinly sliced (if available)

## Process

**Tofu**
1. Press tofu for at least 15 minutes. Toss cubes with soy sauce and sesame oil.
1. Heat neutral oil in a skillet over medium-high. Add tofu in a single layer and cook undisturbed 4–5 minutes until deep golden. Flip and repeat. Set aside.

**Soup**
1. Heat olive oil in a Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium. Add cumin, coriander, and turmeric and cook 60–90 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant and slightly darkened.
1. Add onion and a pinch of salt. Cook 6–8 minutes until softened and starting to brown at the edges.
1. Add garlic and ginger. Cook 2 minutes, stirring frequently.
1. Add carrots and celery. Cook 3–4 minutes.
1. Add lentils and frozen cauliflower. Stir to coat in the aromatics for 1 minute.
1. Add water or stock and soy sauce. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a vigorous simmer. Cook uncovered 40–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until lentils are fully tender and cauliflower has broken down into the broth. Add water if it thickens too aggressively.
1. Stir in sesame oil and apple cider vinegar. Taste and adjust salt, vinegar, and white pepper.

**Finish**
1. Add asparagus to the simmering soup and cook 3–4 minutes — tender but still with some snap.
1. Ladle into bowls and top with crispy tofu and scallions.
