## Ingredients

- 3 pork shoulder steaks (bone-in or boneless, ~3/4-1" thick)
- Salt and black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lb (16 oz) sauerkraut, drained (reserve 1/4 cup brine)
- 1/4 cup sauerkraut brine (reserved above)
- 1/2 cup chicken or pork broth
- 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp caraway seeds (optional)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 tsp brown sugar or honey (optional, balances the acid)

## Process

1. Season pork steaks with salt and pepper. Set Instant Pot to Sauté, add olive oil, and sear steaks 2-3 minutes per side until browned. Remove and set aside.
1. Add onion to the pot, sauté 3-4 minutes until softened. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds.
1. Deglaze with the broth, scraping up browned bits.
1. Stir in sauerkraut, reserved brine, vinegar, caraway (if using), bay leaf, and sugar/honey (if using).
1. Nestle pork steaks back into the pot, spooning some sauerkraut mixture over top.
1. Seal lid, pressure cook on High for 35 minutes. Natural release 10 minutes, then quick release remainder.
1. Remove bay leaf. Taste sauerkraut mixture and adjust acidity/sweetness if needed. Serve steaks topped with sauerkraut and pan juices.

**Notes:** All ingredients are pantry-standard except the pork itself and sauerkraut (already on hand per your non-standard list). No nightshades needed for this one regardless of current constraint status.
