## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked black rice (day-old, chilled)
- ⅓ cup cooked quinoa (day-old, chilled), mixed into rice before cooking
- 14 oz firm or extra-firm tofu, pressed and cut into ¾-inch cubes
- 2 cups frozen cauliflower, squash, and carrot mix
- 1 cup frozen green beans, cut into 1-inch pieces if whole
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, minced
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp Chinkiang vinegar or a small amount of balsamic vinegar
- Neutral oil (or sesame oil cut with neutral) for wok

## Process

1. Combine chilled black rice and quinoa, breaking up any clumps. Set aside.
1. Heat wok over high heat until smoking. Add frozen cauliflower-squash-carrot mix with no oil. Dry-fry, tossing occasionally, until moisture has cooked off and edges begin to char slightly, 3–4 minutes. Remove and set aside.
1. Dry-fry frozen green beans the same way until blistered and moisture is gone, 2–3 minutes. Remove and set aside.
1. Add 2 tbsp neutral oil to wok. When shimmering and just smoking, add tofu in a single layer. Do not move it. Sear until deeply golden on at least two sides, 4–5 minutes total. Remove and set aside.
1. Add 1 tbsp oil to wok. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry 20–30 seconds until fragrant but not browned.
1. Add beaten eggs. Scramble briefly, pulling them around the wok until just set but still slightly wet.
1. Add rice-quinoa mixture immediately, pressing it into the egg before it fully sets so the egg coats the grains. Spread into an even layer and let sit undisturbed 30–45 seconds to develop crust. Toss and repeat 2–3 times.
1. Return vegetables and tofu to wok. Add soy sauce and Shaoxing wine around the perimeter of the wok. Toss everything to combine.
1. Remove from heat. Add sesame oil and Chinkiang vinegar, toss once more. Taste and adjust soy sauce.
1. Serve immediately.
