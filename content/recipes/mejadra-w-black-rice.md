Adapted from *Jerusalem* by Yotam Ottolenghi and Sami Tamimi.
Black rice and quinoa replace basmati; cooked separately due to divergent cook times.
French's crispy fried onions replace the deep-fried onion step.

## Ingredients

**Lentils & Grains**
- ¾ cup green or brown lentils, picked over and rinsed
- ½ cup black rice & quinoa mix (~8:1), rinsed

**Spices & Seasoning**
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tbsp coriander seeds
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ¾ tsp allspice
- ¾ tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp sugar
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

**Other**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ¾ cup water (for the lentil/rice pot)
- French's crispy fried onions, very generous amount — this is the soul of the dish
- Plain whole-milk yogurt, for serving (optional)

## Process

1. **Cook the black rice and quinoa.** Cook the black rice and quinoa in the rice cooker. Fluff and set aside.

1. **Cook the lentils.** Place lentils in a saucepan, cover with plenty of water, bring to a boil, and cook 12–15 minutes until softened but still with a little bite. Drain and set aside.

1. **Bloom the spices.** In a large skillet or saucepan over medium heat, warm the olive oil. Add cumin and coriander seeds and toast 1–2 minutes until fragrant. Add turmeric, allspice, cinnamon, sugar, ½ tsp salt, and plenty of black pepper. Stir 30 seconds.

1. **Combine.** Add lentils, black rice, and quinoa to the pan and fold together until everything is coated and warmed through.

1. **Serve.** Transfer to a shallow bowl. Fold in half the French's onions, pile the rest on top. Serve with yogurt alongside if desired.
